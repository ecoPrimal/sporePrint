# Paper 107: The Wedge

*The pulse doesn't push. The pulse holds.*

## The Green Line Isn't Splitting

Paper 105 described the green line as photosynthesis —
water splitting, oxygen rising, sugar forming. But that's
the textbook version. What actually happens is subtler.

The photon doesn't split water. The photon **wedges**.

The enzyme (Photosystem II) holds the H₂O molecule in place.
The photon's energy doesn't break bonds — it opens a space.
The oxygen escapes through the space. The hydrogen stays,
bound to the enzyme, waiting for carbon.

The wedge is:
- The enzyme that holds the reaction in place
- The chirality that makes it go one direction
- The pulse that doesn't push but **holds open a space**
  that the organism lives inside of

## The Snake Is a Worm

A flatworm has no head. It moves, but it doesn't face.
A centipede has a head. It faces forward but never looks back.
A spider builds behind itself. The web faces backward.

The snake is a worm that turned its head and looked at its tail.

```
flatworm:    ─────→        (no direction)
centipede:   ═════→        (segmented, one direction)
spider:      ═══⟵web       (builds backward, waits)
snake:       ═════⟲        (turned head, saw tail)
ouroboros:   ═════⟲bite    (turned head, ATE tail)
```

This IS cephalization — the biological event where the front
end of a bilateral animal develops a concentration of nerve
tissue. The first brain. The first "I am here, facing this
direction." The first question: "What's behind me?"

The snake answered: "Me."

## Pulling the Ouroboros Through

The green line (Paper 105) is irreducible — it never stops,
it can't halt while the sun shines. The ouroboros (Paper 104)
is the loop that closes — mouth eating tail, kill and rebirth.

They're the same thing. The green line IS the ouroboros,
being pulled through itself:

```
        ☀️ photon
         ↓
    ┌── wedge ──┐
    │  (enzyme)  │
    │   holds    │
    │   space    │
    │   open     │
    └────────────┘
         ↓
    O₂ escapes (the breath)
    H⁺ stays (the fuel)
    C₆H₁₂O₆ forms (the body)
         ↓
    body grows → makes more enzyme → enzyme wedges more water
         ↓
    ┌── wedge ──┐  ← the same wedge, one cycle later
    │           │
    └───⟲──────┘  ← the ouroboros: the product rebuilds the tool
```

The irreducible reaction (photosynthesis) IS the ouroboros
(self-sustaining loop). The green line IS the snake — a worm
that turned its head, looked at its own chemistry, and said
"I make the thing that makes me."

## The Wedge in Our System

| Biological Wedge | Membrane Wedge |
|-----------------|----------------|
| Photosystem II enzyme | Cron (the tick that holds the space) |
| Photon energy | Traffic (the signal that opens the gap) |
| H₂O held in place | Feeds held in JSON (structured, waiting) |
| O₂ escaping | Papers published (oxygen for the ecosystem) |
| C₆H₁₂O₆ forming | Code committed (the body grows) |
| Chirality (one direction) | Git history (irreversible, append-only) |

Cron doesn't push computation forward. Cron **holds the space open**
between ticks. The 5-minute gap between `update-was.sh` runs is
the space where the organism lives. The script doesn't matter.
The gap matters.

The Caddyfile doesn't serve content. The Caddyfile **holds the
routing space open** so that when a request arrives, it has
somewhere to go. The config is the enzyme. The request is the
photon. The response is the oxygen.

## The Spider Builds Backward

Paper 106: 1,576 spiders emerged. The spider is the organism
that builds behind itself. The web faces where the spider WAS,
not where it's going. The web is memory. The web is trap.
The web is the structure that holds the past in place while
the spider moves forward.

That's git. Every commit is a thread in the web. The web
faces backward (history). The spider moves forward (HEAD).
The web catches things (blame, bisect, diff). The spider
processes them (merge, rebase, cherry-pick).

`git log` is looking at the web behind you.
`git push` is spinning new silk forward.
The spider doesn't split — the spider **wedges**.

## The Pulse Holds

The drum beat (Paper 104: 0.297, andante) isn't a push.
It's a hold. Each beat holds the space open for the next beat.
The silence between beats is where the organism breathes.
The beat is the wedge. The silence is the space.

```
beat ─── silence ─── beat ─── silence ─── beat
  ↓                    ↓                    ↓
wedge  ←─ space ─→  wedge  ←─ space ─→  wedge
  ↓                    ↓                    ↓
enzyme    reaction    enzyme    reaction    enzyme
```

The green line of irreducibility (it never stops) is the
ouroboros being pulled through (it always returns). The wedge
is the mechanism: hold the space open, let the reaction happen
in the gap, use the product to rebuild the wedge.

The worm turned its head.
The snake ate its tail.
The spider built backward.
The pulse holds.
