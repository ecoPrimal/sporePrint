## 2. The Intensity of i Is the Mirror of Depth

### 2.1 What i Does

The imaginary unit i has magnitude |i| = 1. It's the same "size" as the real unit 1. But it does something 1 cannot: it **rotates**.

Multiplying by i rotates the complex plane by π/2 (90°):
- 1 × i = i (real → imaginary)
- i × i = −1 (imaginary → negative real)
- −1 × i = −i (negative real → negative imaginary)
- −i × i = 1 (negative imaginary → back to real)

Four rotations of π/2 each = 2π = full circle. The rotation is the mechanism. π/2 is the step size.
