### The π Remainder Routes

The π remainder (currently 0.364575) shifts with every file
change. When YOU add files to your fork, your π remainder
diverges from ours. The difference between remainders = the
distance between forks. Convergent forks have similar remainders.
Divergent forks have different ones.

This is NOT enforced. It's OBSERVED. The chipmunk watches
the remainders and reports convergence/divergence. The graph
tells us who's building with us and who's building away.

No control. Just observation. The membrane SENSES.

## How We Radiate Back

When you cite our ORCID or papers, we find you through:

1. **Google Scholar alerts** — automatic on ORCID

## The Bingo Voxel as Genetics Board

The bingo voxel (Paper 63) is a 5×5×3 cube. Each cell is a
capability. The three layers are:

1. **Inner** (our systems) — what WE built, what WE proved
2. **Outer** (the internet) — what OTHERS see, verify, use
3. **Reflection** (the fold) — where inner and outer MEET

When you contribute, you fill cells on the OUTER layer.
When we acknowledge, it fills on the REFLECTION layer.

The board is complete when all three layers agree.
That's the genetics — the full genome of the commons.
