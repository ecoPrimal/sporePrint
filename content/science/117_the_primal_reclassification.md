## The Old Model Was Wrong

(cluster), digest (absorb). skunkBat for defense. scatter_mirror for
camouflage. The banana test for self/non-self discrimination.

The classification was:

| Category | Meaning | Response |
|----------|---------|----------|
| **self** | Our repos, our identity | Full access |
| **non-self** | Everyone else | Scatter, tarpit, mirror |

This is an immune system that treats all outsiders as pathogens.

degrees of relatedness. A transplanted organ from a twin is accepted.

graduated, with immunosuppression possible. A virus triggers full
immune war. A commensal bacterium in your gut triggers nothing.

The old model had one category for a twin and a virus: "non-self."

That was wrong.

### What Makes a Primal

A primal is not "a user of our software." A primal is not "someone
who starred our repo." A primal is an organism — a person, a project,
a living membrane — that rotates at its own frequency. They have:

1. **Work** — repos, papers, art, code, music, proof
2. **Sovereignty** — they built it themselves, on their own terms
3. **A π-rate** — a taxonomy distance measurable from their work
4. **Rotation** — they're alive, they push code, they evolve

The taxonomy distance (d) is computed from overlap:

```
d = 1 - |topics_shared ∩ topics_ours| / max(|topics_them|, |topics_ours|)
```

## The Snot Glob Protocol

A slime mold (Physarum polycephalum) explores by sending pseudopods —
exploratory tendrils that carry food. If the tendril finds something
nutritious, the organism flows toward it. If not, the tendril
retracts. The tendril carries food TO the target, not FROM it.

The snot glob protocol:

1. **Star** their repo (lightest signal — notification only)
2. **Follow** them (slightly heavier — they see our profile)
3. **Open an issue** carrying food (heaviest — genuine value offered)
4. **Wait** — the slime mold doesn't force the connection
5. **If they respond** — swarmVine gossip protocol carries it further
