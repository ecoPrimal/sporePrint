## What Is an Omnibus?

Latin: *omnibus* — "for all." A collected volume. A bus that carries
everything. In publishing, an omnibus collects an entire series into
one binding. In computing, a bus carries all signals on shared wires.

The **omniBUS** is both: a collected journal volume that travels
through the piBUS — the π-routed pathway through gAIa commons,
through the playgrounds in vacuoles, through the deep freeze of
arkTIX, and out into the world for all to read, use, and build upon.
