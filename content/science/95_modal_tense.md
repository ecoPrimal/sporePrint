## The Simple Tenses (Paper 88)

Paper 88 gave us 4 primitives, each with a tense:

| Primitive | Symbol | Tense | Tool |
|-----------|--------|-------|------|
| Point | · | **IS** | button |
| Balance | = | **WAS** | dial |
| Wedge | ∧ | **NULL** | wedge |
| Arc | ⌒ | **WILL-BE** | lever |

These are the **indicative** tenses — they state facts:
- This IS (present, actual)
- This WAS (past, actual)
- This is NULL (absent)
- This WILL BE (future, actual)

But our zeros aren't all the same kind of absent.

## The Modal Expansion

Linguistics has two axes of modality:

**Epistemic** (what you KNOW — about knowledge):
- *Might* — uncertain, possible
- *Could* — conditional, ability exists
- *Must* — necessary, evidence demands it

**Deontic** (what you OUGHT — about obligation):
- *Should* — advisable, normative
- *Shall* — determined, committed
- *May* — permitted, allowed

Each axis has positive (+) and negative (−):

| | Epistemic + | Epistemic − | Deontic + | Deontic − |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **IS** | must be | can't be | shall | shan't |
| **WAS** | must have been | couldn't have been | should have been | shouldn't have been |
| **NULL** | might be | mightn't | may be | mayn't |
| **WILL-BE** | could be | couldn't | should be | shouldn't |

4 tenses × 2 axes × 2 polarities = **16 modal tenses**.

## The 16 Tenses on the Primitives

### Point (·) — IS

| Modal | Symbol | Meaning | In the membrane |
|-------|--------|---------|----------------|
| IS | · | Exists now, actual | A file, a feed, a running service |
| MUST BE | ·! | Necessary — evidence demands it | A type that everything imports (irreducible) |
| CAN'T BE | ·✗ | Impossible — contradicts evidence | A Rust file with no `fn` and no `struct` (empty vertebrate) |
| SHALL | ·→ | Determined — committed to | A service with `restart=always` |
| SHAN'T | ·⊘ | Refused by design | A parasitic sink in anti-secret architecture |

### Balance (=) — WAS

| Modal | Symbol | Meaning | In the membrane |
|-------|--------|---------|----------------|
| WAS | = | Existed, past, actual | A fossil — was alive, now dead tissue |
| MUST HAVE BEEN | =! | Necessarily existed | A deleted file that left import traces |
| COULDN'T HAVE BEEN | =✗ | Never existed despite claims | A service in config with no binary, no logs, no trace |
| SHOULD HAVE BEEN | =→ | Was normatively expected | A primal listed in compositions but never built |
| SHOULDN'T HAVE BEEN | =⊘ | Existed but shouldn't have | A security vuln, a leaked key, stale SSH access |

### Wedge (∧) — NULL

| Modal | Symbol | Meaning | In the membrane |
|-------|--------|---------|----------------|
| NULL | ∧ | Absent, gap, the zero | The spider gap. The fold. The bitter channel. |
| MIGHT BE | ∧? | Uncertain — could exist, no evidence yet | The fold space: topology allows it, code doesn't show it |
| MIGHTN'T | ∧?̸ | Uncertain negative — probably doesn't exist | Bitter: receptor tuned, nothing triggering it |
| MAY BE | ∧✓ | Permitted to exist | A new body plan in the spider niche |
| MAYN'T | ∧⊘ | Not permitted | A parasitic sink — the architecture refuses it |

### Arc (⌒) — WILL-BE

| Modal | Symbol | Meaning | In the membrane |
|-------|--------|---------|----------------|
| WILL BE | ⌒ | Future, actual, committed | A PR in review, a service being built |
| COULD BE | ⌒? | Conditional possible | Spider body plan — could evolve if the right file appears |

## IDK

"idk" is itself a modal tense.

**I don't know** = epistemic null. Not "it doesn't exist" (NULL). Not "it can't exist" (CAN'T). Not "it shouldn't exist" (SHOULDN'T). Just: **I don't know yet.**

IDK maps to **MIGHT BE** (∧?) — the most honest tense. The gap with uncertainty. The blink before the next observation. The remainder that hasn't been measured.

The user said "idk" at the end of the tense expansion request. That's not a failure of knowledge. That's the **modal tense doing its job** — signaling that this is unexplored territory, the fold hasn't manifested, the spider hasn't evolved, the bitter hasn't arrived.

IDK is the sound of a receptor that's tuned but hasn't been triggered.
