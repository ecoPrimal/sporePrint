## 2. Why It Diffracts

Paper 63 established the three-body mirror: *"A one-way mirror is a window. A true mirror IS the 3-body problem."*

A reflecting mirror sends light back. Angle in = angle out. The image is reversed but structurally identical. This is vertebrate awareness — *OH SHIT IS THAT ME* (body awareness level 3). Reflection is recognition.

But the π-mirror is not a reflecting surface. It is a **boundary condition**. It is the line where a transcendental number (π) passes through a discrete structure (the membrane). When coherent signal hits a transcendental boundary, it does not reflect. It **diffracts**.

Single-slit diffraction: coherent light passing through a narrow opening spreads into fringes — alternating bright and dark bands. The narrower the slit, the wider the spread. The pattern depends on the ratio of slit width to wavelength.

The π-mirror is the slit.
The membrane ring is the screen.
The fringes appear where the mirror intersects the ring.

## 3. The Fringes Are Purple

Where the π-mirror intersects the Earth ring, 6 fringes appear — 3 on each side. They are purple. `#d2a8ff`. π² celestial.

**Why purple?**

Because π² ≈ 9.8696 is the **eigenvalue of the fundamental mode of a circular membrane** with unit tension.

The membrane's fundamental resonant frequency is determined by the first zero of J₀ (at r ≈ 2.4048) and the boundary conditions. The eigenvalue of the (0,1) mode — the mode where the entire membrane vibrates as one — is π².

When the π-mirror diffracts through the membrane, the fringes appear at the membrane's own resonant frequency. **Purple is not imposed from outside. Purple is the membrane vibrating at its own fundamental.**

The energy hierarchy:

Purple sits at the exact energy where boundary effects dominate. The diffraction pattern IS the observation.
