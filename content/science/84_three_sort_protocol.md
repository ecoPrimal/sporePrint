## Abstract

Three independent sorting systems — Shannon mass (content), BINGO voxel (entities), and π pulse (rhythm) — converge to a single composition protocol shaped like a right triangle whose legs twist. The horizontal leg (L/R) is **pink** — human signal, Shannon entropy, information density flowing left-to-right through pages. The vertical leg (Up/Down) is **green** — structural complexity, voxel confidence, irreducibility growing upward. The hypotenuse is **blue** — identity, the pair, the ratio where reader meets reading. The triangle twists because two lines braid through it: the **green irreducibility line** (you cannot compress structure past this floor) and the **brown loam line** (permanence — what loamSpine has committed). Their twist is the twiristina — from Tina, the inner explorer who reads everything, the inner surface of the tube topology.

Each sort classifies a different dimension of the same system:

None replaces the others. Each is a different projection of the same system.

## 2. The Right Triangle

The three sorts map to the **pulse family** — the membrane's noise colors:

```
        green (3) — structure, complexity, irreducibility
        |
        |    ╱ blue (2) — identity, the pair, hypotenuse
        |   ╱
        |  ╱
        | ╱
        |╱___________  pink (1) — human signal, entropy, content
```

### 2.1 Horizontal Leg: Pink (L/R)

**Pink** = the human signal. Strength = human fraction / 15% (full pink).

This is the Shannon sort axis. Information flows **left to right** through pages. Low-entropy pages sit left (low mass). High-entropy pages sit right (high mass). The horizontal sweep of a reader moving through content IS the pink signal.

- **Value now**: 0.1242 (12.42% of full human presence)
- **Shannon maps here**: H̄ = 7.33 bits mean entropy across 1,386 pages
- **Direction**: L→R as mass increases, R→L as reader returns to simpler content

### 2.2 Vertical Leg: Green (Up/Down)

**Green** = the tree, structure, complexity. Strength = structural complexity measure.

This is the voxel sort axis. Entities are classified **bottom to top** by confidence. Null entities sit at the bottom (unclassified). High-confidence self/non-self sit at the top. The vertical rise of classification IS the green signal.

- **Value now**: 0.4622 (46.22% structural complexity)
- **Voxel maps here**: 97.3% confidence, 342 holdouts remain at the bottom
- **Direction**: Up as confidence increases, Down when new unclassified entities appear

**Green is the irreducibility line**: you cannot compress structure past this floor. A 7×7×3 voxel cube with 97.3% fill is nearly irreducible — the remaining 2.7% is the true mystery that no algorithm resolves alone. Green measures how close to that floor you are.

### 2.3 Hypotenuse: Blue (the pair)

**Blue** = identity match, the pair, human + artisan together. Strength = how close the remainder is to 2/π.

This is the π sort. The hypotenuse connects content (pink) to structure (green) through identity (blue). When a high-confidence entity reads a high-mass page, the blue signal strengthens — the pair is formed.

- **Value now**: 0.7834 (78.34% identity match)
- **π maps here**: remainder = 0.774493, target = 2/π = 0.6366
- **Direction**: the diagonal from (0,0) to (pink, green) — the identity IS the composition

## 3. The Twist: Green × Brown = Twiristina

The right triangle doesn't stay flat. Two lines twist through it:

### 3.1 The Green Line (irreducibility)

The green line runs **vertically** through the triangle. It marks the floor of structural complexity — the point below which the system cannot be compressed without losing information. In Shannon terms, this is the entropy rate. In voxel terms, this is the minimum confidence for classification. In π terms, this is the irrational remainder that never resolves.

Green rises as:
- More entities get classified (voxel sort confidence grows)
- Page structure deepens (more cross-links, more citations)
- The membrane accumulates observations (fossil count grows)

Green can never reach 1.0 — irreducibility has a ceiling. The 342 holdouts are permanent mysteries. Some fraction of the system is always unknown.

### 3.2 The Brown Line (permanence / loam)

The brown line runs **horizontally** through the triangle. It marks what loamSpine has committed — the permanent record. Everything below the brown line is ephemeral (rhizoCrypt sessions, in-flight braids). Everything above it is permanent (DataAnchors, BraidCommits).

Brown grows as:
- Pages get content-hashed and anchored (Blake3 → DataAnchor)
- Braids get committed (BraidCommit with subject_hash)
- Mass weights get written into frontmatter (the weight IS a commitment)

Brown is monotonically non-decreasing — loam never forgets. The only direction is accumulation.

### 3.3 The Twist

The green line (vertical, irreducibility) and brown line (horizontal, permanence) **twist around each other** like a double helix:

```
    green ──╲ ╱── green ──╲ ╱── green
             ╳             ╳
    brown ──╱ ╲── brown ──╱ ╲── brown
```

Each sort pass advances both:
1. Shannon sort writes weights (brown advances — permanence)
2. Shannon sort measures entropy (green advances — irreducibility known)
3. Voxel sort classifies entities (green advances — structure)
4. Voxel sort pushes to graph (brown advances — committed)
5. π pulse measures rhythm (the twist rate — how fast the helix turns)

**The twist rate IS the pulse remainder** (0.774). The helix turns at 0.774 rotations per membrane cycle. It never completes a full turn — the irrational remainder means the twist never repeats exactly. Every pass sees the system from a slightly different angle.

This is **Tina** — the inner explorer who reads everything, wired as the inner surface of the tube topology. The twiristina is the tube's inner surface twisting: you read the content (pink, L→R) while the structure rises around you (green, ↑), and identity forms on the diagonal (blue, ↗). The twist is what makes the tube a tube and not a flat sheet.

## 4. The Protocol: Automatic Handling

As sorts run iteratively, the protocol handles more automatically:

### 4.1 Per-Pass Actions

```
PASS N:
  1. SCAN  — squirrel scans membrane (pink input)
  2. WEIGH — sourdough validate mass (pink → green: entropy measured)
  3. SORT  — voxel cube classifies (green ↑: confidence grows)
  4. ANCHOR — Blake3 hash committed (brown →: permanence grows)
  5. TWIST — π pulse measures rhythm (blue ↗: identity updates)
  6. EVOLVE — if green > threshold: fossilize observation
```

### 4.2 Convergence

The triangle converges when:
- **Pink** saturates (all pages weighted, no new content)
- **Green** saturates (97.3% → approaching irreducibility ceiling)
- **Blue** saturates (identity match → 2/π ≈ 0.6366)
- **Brown** catches up to green (everything irreducible is also permanent)

Full convergence is impossible (the remainder is irrational). But the twist tightens — each pass brings green and brown closer together, reducing the gap between "what we know" and "what we've committed."

### 4.3 What the Sorts Handle Automatically

| Pass | Shannon handles | Voxel handles | π handles |
|------|----------------|---------------|-----------|
| 1 | Weight assignment | Keyword classification | Rhythm measurement |
| 2 | Delta detection (hash changed?) | Neighbor consensus | Identity match |
| 3 | Flat-scan fallback | BINGO cube fill | Pulse normalization |
| N+1 | Only re-weigh changed pages | Only re-sort low-confidence entities | Twist rate trending |

### 4.4 The Loam Composition Rule

From sweetGrass braid compression: **0 / 1 / Many**.

- **0**: No data → null (don't classify, don't weight, don't anchor)
- **1**: One observation → provisional (weight assigned, entity classified, but not committed)
- **Many**: Multiple concordant observations → permanent (loamSpine DataAnchor, irreducible)

The protocol never takes one and leaves the other. Both sorts run. Both results compose. The twist ensures they see different angles of the same system.
