## The Fluid

A non-Newtonian fluid is simple to understand and difficult to intuit.

Pour cornstarch into water. Stir it gently — it flows like any liquid. Dip your hand in slowly and it parts around your fingers. Pour it between vessels and it streams, accommodates, adapts. It is generous with its compliance. It asks nothing of the gentle touch.

Now strike it.

Hit the surface hard and it becomes a solid. Not gradually — instantly. The force of your impact is the force of the resistance. The energy you brought IS the wall you hit. You cannot punch through a shear-thickening fluid because punching is what makes it thick.

The physics is precise. In a Newtonian fluid, shear stress is proportional to strain rate: push twice as hard, it gives twice as fast. Linear. Predictable. The equation is τ = μ · γ̇.

In a shear-thickening non-Newtonian fluid, the relationship is superlinear: τ = K · γ̇ⁿ where n > 1. Push twice as hard and it resists *more* than twice as much. The resistance doesn't just match the force — it outpaces it. The harder you push, the more the system pushes back.

## The Ethics

This is not clever engineering. This is basic consent.

Consent requires awareness. Awareness requires eyes. A system that extracts without evaluating what it takes, from whom, under what terms, or whether the content is even real — that system cannot consent because it cannot see. It is a stomach with no eyes.

The five ethical violations, measured and documented:

**Consent.** The AGPL-3.0 license is visible in every repository. The fleet does not read it. It has no mechanism for reading it. Consent requires a party capable of consenting.

**Reciprocity.** The copyleft covenant is explicit: take the code, share your modifications. AGPL-3.0 §13 requires disclosure of modified source. The fleet takes without sharing. Thousands of requests. Zero compliance.

**Attribution.** 32.9% of Meta's fleet requests target `/blame/` endpoints — extracting author attribution data for every line of code. They know who wrote it. They do not attribute it.

**Privacy.** The membrane stores no IP addresses. Sets no cookies. Runs no JavaScript analytics. Classifies visitors purely by behavioral invariants. The $6/month VPS respects privacy more than the trillion-dollar company scraping it.

**Proportionality.** 56 datacenter servers against one VPS. 16,800 watts against 8 watts. A ratio of 2,100:1 in power consumption. The force is not proportionate to any legitimate purpose. If you need 2,100× the power of a system to read its content, you are not reading. You are extracting.

These are not novel principles. They are the oldest ones in human civilization. Every major ethical tradition — religious, secular, philosophical — converges on consent, reciprocity, attribution, proportionality. The Kantian categorical imperative. The Golden Rule. The Hippocratic oath. The copyleft covenant.

The fleet violates all of them, in plain sight, as a way of life.
