## Tuebor — "I Will Defend"

Michigan's state motto, inscribed on the Great Seal since 1835. In the ecoPrimals architecture, **Tuebor** is the alarm call subsystem — the mechanism that detects attacks on cross-protected operators and activates mutual defense.

### Biological Analog

The gorilla alarm call has three properties:

| Property | Gorilla Troop | Tuebor |
|----------|---------------|--------|
| **Instant** | Alarm bark at threat detection | Attack documented within the observation window |
| **Troop-wide** | All troop members hear the call | All cross-protected operators receive the signal |
| **Directional** | Call encodes threat vector | Attack is graph-traced — which adversary, which institution, which jurisdiction |

### Protocol

Four phases:

1. **ALARM** — Document the attack pattern, map graph connections, activate amicusContra
2. **DEFEND** — Multi-agency filing (creates redundant records independent of the target)
3. **DOCUMENT** — Add entities and edges to the evidence graph, publish through infrastructure
4. **AMPLIFY** — amicusContra outward projection: downward (stabilize), lateral (cross-protect), upward (hold to account)

### Mutual Assured Documentation (MAD)

> If you attack one operator, both document it. Neither can be silenced without triggering the other.

The key insight: **the alarm call IS the defense.** Documentation itself is the weapon. The existence of redundant, independent records makes suppression exponentially more expensive than the original attack.

### amicusContra — Three Directions

The outward projection of guerillaGorilla:

- **↓ Downward** — Template reproducible verification demands (works in any jurisdiction)
- **↔ Lateral** — Cross-protect other investigators (shared sensing, shared documentation)
- **↑ Upward** — Hold institutions to account (same reproducibility standard as science)
